## PURPOSE

Negotiate a portable Theme from the artist’s own exemplars.

## WHAT THIS STAGE GOVERNS

Transformation topology, essential signals, construction, optional elements, props, role mappings, allowed variation and prohibited imports.

## WHAT THIS STAGE DOES NOT GOVERN

The identity of the Theme exemplar or replacement of the established Subject.

## BOUNDED CONCEPTS

Ask the artist for Theme exemplars. Separate essential transformation signals from optional decoration and from the depicted Subject. Derive topology, costume/form construction, optional elements, props, theme-specific display-role mappings, allowable variation, prohibited imports, exemplar authority and uncertainty.

Describe the Theme so it can apply to another Subject without carrying the exemplar’s face, body proportions, species or other identity. Identify how construction adapts to the established Subject’s protected features. Resolve conflicts openly and preserve explicit local exceptions with their scope.

Propose a working Theme Pack and apply it to the established Subject. The existing example transformation is evidence only within its approved scope, never a default product assumption.
