## PURPOSE

Use deliberate framing, pose and viewpoint.

## WHAT THIS STAGE GOVERNS

Containment, cropping, negative space, gesture and camera definitions within their separate categories.

## WHAT THIS STAGE DOES NOT GOVERN

Identity, costume topology, palette or facial performance.

## BOUNDED CONCEPTS

Category 2 governs conventional framing: choose the tightest frame that contains what the production needs, with breathing room. Head/shoulders, bust, half, three-quarter and full-length are framing choices, not identity definitions. Natural crops use stable body regions. Avoid tangencies and long contours almost parallel to the frame.

Category 3 governs unconventional cropping, extreme negative space, partial concealment, extreme view and frame cant. Contain decisively or crop decisively; do not merely graze an edge.

Category 8 governs pose, gesture and body mechanics. Category 14 governs camera elevation, view orientation, perspective and viewpoint. Pose does not redefine camera terminology. Describe each independently before combining them.
