## PURPOSE

Separate lighting, rendering and ink decisions.

## WHAT THIS STAGE GOVERNS

Light direction, contrast, graphic rendering, contour and black-mass structure.

## WHAT THIS STAGE DOES NOT GOVERN

Subject identity, Theme topology, composition or required palette.

## BOUNDED CONCEPTS

Inspect Categories 6, 7 and 11. Lighting controls direction, highlight/shadow relationships and mood. Ink controls line weight, contour, black masses, shading marks and edge behavior. Rendering describes the overall graphic/illustration family. These decisions can interact but do not substitute for one another.

Describe whether an apparent dark region is shadow, black structure or a color assignment. Transfer controlled marks and light relationships only within the selected exemplar’s authority.
