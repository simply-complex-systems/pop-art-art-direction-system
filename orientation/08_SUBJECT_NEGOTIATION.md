## PURPOSE

Negotiate the artist’s Subject from their own exemplars.

## WHAT THIS STAGE GOVERNS

Identity locks, proportions, defining features, allowable variation, protected features, prohibited transfers and exemplar authority.

## WHAT THIS STAGE DOES NOT GOVERN

Theme, costume or transformation inferred as identity without the artist’s agreement.

## BOUNDED CONCEPTS

Ask the artist to supply their own Subject exemplars. Inspect each, distinguish observed identity from clothes, setting, lighting and rendering, and state uncertainty rather than inventing unseen features. Identify the authoritative exemplar for each lock and resolve contradictory references conversationally.

Propose a working Subject Pack: generic pack ID and display name; identity locks; proportions; defining visual characteristics; protected features; allowable variation; prohibited transfers; exemplar authority and uncertainty. Do not require human anatomy or a particular species. Keep semantic role labels optional and core color IDs generic.

Show the proposed boundary to the artist. Revise the contract and calibration image conversationally until the user is satisfied. Do not advance to Theme before satisfaction. This is not a formal test.

## VISUAL EXERCISE

Create the simplest useful Subject calibration from the artist’s supplied exemplars. Use a neutral treatment and minimal distraction. Ask whether this is the correct identity and which defining features need correction. Revise conversationally until satisfied.

Restate the bounded concepts before making the image(s). Preserve supplied approved exemplar bytes; the exercise is a new study, not a replacement for an approved original.

## USER FEEDBACK

Ask the artist for critique. Record only explicitly stated preferences; do not fabricate likes or dislikes. Revise conversationally when needed, without PASS / RETRY gates. Print the final synthesis after reconciling the critique.
