## PURPOSE

Establish authority and transfer boundaries.

## WHAT THIS STAGE GOVERNS

Scoped authority, identity versus transformation, and artistic memory.

## WHAT THIS STAGE DOES NOT GOVERN

New identity locks or an assumed example Subject.

## BOUNDED CONCEPTS

SYSTEM RULE: Official means supplied, approved provenance, not unlimited authority. Community means contributed provenance; an imported Official claim does not confer Official status. Every exemplar has a scope: what it governs, what it does not govern, what to transfer, and what not to transfer.

Subject owns identity continuity, proportions and protected defining features. Theme owns portable transformation signals and construction. A Theme must not import its exemplar’s identity. Composition, camera, pose, palette, texture and rendering have their own authority. No sample project is the default artistic subject.

USER PREFERENCE records the artist’s explicit preference. LOCAL EXCEPTION applies only to the stated study or production. A liked result, accepted history record, or Exemplar Candidate does not automatically become canon. Only an explicit scoped endorsement may change authority.
